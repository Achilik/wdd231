Bekpels' Fashion Empire

Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Bekpels' Fashion Empire

This name was selected because it represents a professional fashion school that trains aspiring designers and fashion entrepreneurs. It is memorable, brand-oriented, and clearly communicates the purpose of the organization.

Optional Domain: www.bekpelsfashionempire.com

Site Purpose

The website will provide information about fashion design programs, admission requirements, tuition fees, student projects, and contact information. Visitors will be able to learn about the school, explore student work, and apply for admission online.

Scenarios

  • What fashion design courses are available?
  • How do I apply for admission?
  • What are the tuition fees and payment options?
  • How can I contact the school for more information?
