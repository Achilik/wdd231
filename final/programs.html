Programs Offered

Fashion Design

Learn fashion illustration, color theory, textile selection, garment styling, and modern fashion trends. Students develop creative concepts and transform ideas into professional designs.

Pattern Drafting

Master the technical skills required to create accurate patterns for dresses, skirts, trousers, shirts, and custom garments.

Garment Construction

Gain practical experience using industrial sewing equipment, fabric cutting techniques, garment assembly, and finishing methods.

Fashion Entrepreneurship

Learn branding, customer service, digital marketing, pricing, and business management to launch and grow a successful fashion brand.
